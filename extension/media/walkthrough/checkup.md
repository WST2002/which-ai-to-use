## What wastes your tokens

Your AI reads files while it searches. A `package-lock.json` alone can be 50,000+ tokens, and every add-on (MCP server) is sent with each message.

The checkup lists them in plain words, each with one fix, and gives your project an **AI setup score** out of 100.

Heavy files can be skipped in one click: **Fix it now**. Your code is never touched; only your AI tools' ignore settings change.
