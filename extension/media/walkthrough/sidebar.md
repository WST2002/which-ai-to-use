## Everything in one place

Click the **✦ Which AI** icon on the left edge of VS Code. The sidebar shows:

- your **AI setup score** and the tokens your AI may waste
- **Checkup**, **Fix wasted tokens** and **Which AI for a task?** as buttons
- **Claude limit over?** to hand the task to another AI
- your **usage**, with when Claude's limit resets

Too narrow? Click **Full view** to open it in an editor tab.
